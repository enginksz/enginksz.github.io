I work on **perception** and **localization** for machines that operate off the map: unstructured terrain and GNSS-degraded air. C++, ROS 2, and Jetson. I do not build the vehicle.

I am a robotics software engineer at **HAVELSAN — Robotics and Autonomous Systems**. Employer UGV and UAV programs are confidential. I own perception and localization pieces — I do not lead the platform.

Public work is listed under [Projects](/projects/). SIU 2026 is the field paper. Open-benchmark air and ground localization pages are for reproducible eval without employer data. An uncertainty-aware off-road semantics dataset project is in progress; it is not the employer stack.

## What I can show

- Field UAV map-aided localization vs RTK — [SIU 2026](/projects/gnss_denied_localization/)
- Public UAV geo-localization — [OrthoLoC + AerialVL](/projects/public_uav_geoloc/)
- Public ground localization — [MulRan / UrbanNav, GPS on vs denied](/projects/public_ground_loc/)
- Perception demos (follow-me, etc.) under Projects; not a substitute for the localization pages above

## Focus

- Visual and LiDAR–inertial localization, including GNSS-denied stretches
- Multi-modal sensor fusion (camera, LiDAR, IMU, GNSS when available)
- Onboard vision: segmentation and tracking, TensorRT on Jetson
- Map-aided UAV localization against satellite / orthophoto geodata ([SIU 2026](/projects/gnss_denied_localization/), [public benchmarks](/projects/public_uav_geoloc/))
